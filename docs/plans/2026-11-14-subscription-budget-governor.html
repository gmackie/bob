Subscription budget governor & Budget cockpit Design approved

2026-11-14gmackie/bob @ bob-76-budget-governorBOB-76Claude

Recommendation: Build a pure budget governor into Bob's existing autoDrain tick. It reads each subscription's own weekly and 5-hour meters through the runner host, attributes spend with CLIProxyAPI's per-key ledger, and paces Bob's autonomous work to fill each account to its landing target by reset without touching the reserve kept for your interactive sessions. Steering happens from a new /budget cockpit whose levers preview their effect live before you commit them.

Overview

CLIProxyAPI already fronts six subscription accounts (2× Claude, 2× Codex, Kimi, xAI), and Bob already routes every runner path through it. Proxy health is already visible on the Nodes page and in the mobile app (2026-09-20 plan, Phases 1–2). Bob also has a cron-driven backlog driver (packages/bob/src/api/src/handlers/autoDrain.ts) that paces a daily run count (services/health/pacing.ts).

That leaves four gaps:

  • Nothing knows how much of a subscription's weekly budget is left. Bob only sees a cooldown after a limit has already been hit.
  • Pacing counts runs, not budget, and ignores each account's own reset time.
  • Budget left over at the end of the week is wasted instead of being turned into planning.
  • There's nowhere to see or steer any of this.

Decisions made

Authority
Fill the remainder

The governor steers only Bob-dispatched work. Your interactive sessions are measured and forecast, never throttled.

Measurement
Meters + ledger

Provider 5-hour and weekly meters are the source of truth. The CLIProxyAPI usage ledger provides attribution and estimates for Kimi and xAI.

Surplus
Full ladder + BizPulse

Grooming, pulling work forward, discovering new issues, and BizPulse venture work. Each tier can be switched on or off and ordered; "lapse" is the floor.

Hook point (revised after Phase 0)
Shared module, both dispatchers

Production runs on legacy autoDrain, which sits at its flat 40 runs/day cap almost every day. The durable coordinator (packages/bob/coordination) had 0 dispatches in 7 days. The governor is one shared pure module: autoDrain uses it now in place of the flat cap, and the coordinator later gates dispatchOne by executionProfileId.

Surface
Web cockpit + mobile glance

A full /budget page in apps/bob, plus gauges, mode control and pushes on mobile Home.

Goals & non-goals

Goals

  • Each subscription ends its week within ±5% of its landing target.
  • Your interactive reserve and 5-hour headroom are never consumed by Bob.
  • Work is routed to whichever subscription has budget, not round-robin.
  • Every work item carries its cost in tokens and in % of a week.
  • A 4-week forecast of what the backlog will absorb, per project.
  • Levers you can understand before you pull them.

Non-goals

  • Throttling or rerouting interactive traffic at the proxy.
  • Replacing CLIProxyAPI's own control panel.
  • Metered API-key spend (dollar budgets). Subscriptions only.
  • Autonomous durable writes from surplus tiers. They produce proposals.
  • Rewriting autoDrain's work selection beyond adding tiers and making routing budget-aware.

Attribution is exact, not inferred. Bob runners already authenticate to the proxy with a dedicated CLIPROXY_API_KEY, and CLIProxyAPI reports usage by API key. So every token spent on that key is Bob's and everything else is interactive. The ledger then adds tier and work-item tags from Bob's own run records.

Pacing math — try it

For each account on each tick: forecast interactive use to reset (Î), compute spendable budget S = max(0, landing − used − reserve − Î), and spread S evenly until reset − landing buffer. In the final release window, unused reserve unlocks gradually. Dividing by the measured cost per run converts the rate into run slots. The 5-hour ceiling then zeroes an account's slots for any tick where its short window is too hot.

Surplus ladder

Delivery always has first claim. Only budget the governor projects will go unused flows down the ladder.

  1. Deliveryready/todo Kanbanger issues through the existing dispatch path.
  2. Groom & planSpecs, acceptance criteria, estimates and splits for backlog issues. Proposals move them to todo. Unestimated items come first.
  3. Pull forwardDispatch backlog items that are normally held behind the manual gate.
  4. DiscoverySurvey repos (tech debt, test gaps, failing CI, TODOs) and propose new issues.
  5. BizPulseGrowth, compliance, devops and research personas on startup ventures, through packages/ooda/src/integrations/bizpulse-adapter.ts proposals.
  6. LapseThe floor: anything still unallocated is left unused.

Draft → preview → commit

The governor ships as a shared pure module, so the browser reruns it on every lever drag and redraws the ghost projection. Commit shows the diff and its predicted impact, and logs it with an author.

Everything clicks through

Gauge slices open runs, ladder rows open Kanbanger issues or BizPulse proposals, and each decision opens the full inputs for its tick.

Honest confidence

Values estimated from the ledger get a hatched fill and an "est." label. Stale meters turn grey with their age. Forecasts show a range, not a single number.

Fast hands

P pause, B boost, ⌘⏎ commit, with a 10-second undo toast. Pause and Boost both expire on their own. Respects reduced motion; light and dark themes.

Throughput per project

Done vs planned this week, cost as % of a week, median cost per item, and a 4-week trend for each Kanbanger project and BizPulse venture.

Capacity planner

Weeks W+0 to W+4, each sized to that week's net capacity and filled by priority × project weight with estimated item costs. It shows backlog depth in weeks; "Commit plan" writes target cycles to Kanbanger.

Cost per work item

Runs record tokens from the agent CLI's own usage events into token_usage_sessions (packages/bob/src/schema/src/agents.ts:196), tagged with account, model, tier and work item. A per-account conversion rate (meter % per weighted token) is fitted each week from meter movement against ledger tokens, which gives every item a cost in % of a week summed across retries and review fixes. When an item closes, its cost is written back to the Kanbanger issue. Estimates for backlog items come from similar past items (same project, type, size label), shown as a range with a confidence level. Backlog depth under 1 week of capacity shifts surplus toward grooming and discovery.

Mitigations
  • R1 Meters are provider response headers captured by the proxy fork, not a documented API. Header names are pinned in fixtures. A missing or renamed signal marks the meter stale and the governor falls back to the ledger with a 10% safety margin. It fails closed. Phase 0 found no need for the unofficial OAuth usage endpoints.
  • R7 Ledger loss: queue records expire after 60s. Poll every ≤20s, alert when a poll gap exceeds 45s, and consider raising redis-usage-queue-retention-seconds on the proxy (a config change on labnuc, needs approval).
  • R8 Terms of service: Anthropic's published terms (Feb 2026) restrict using subscription OAuth tokens in third-party tools. That applies to the existing CLIProxyAPI setup as a whole, which this plan builds on but doesn't change. The governor raises usage volume on that setup.
  • R2 Bob could starve your interactive sessions. The 5-hour ceiling, the reserve, and a reserve-breach push guard against it; Pause takes effect on the next tick; Boost can't cross either guard.
  • R3 Concurrent runs blur meter deltas. Per-run tokens come from the CLIs; the conversion rate is fitted weekly across many runs, not per run.
  • R4 Interactive use is bursty. Use an hour-of-week EWMA with a confidence band, and project with the upper band while the week is young.
  • R5 Surplus tiers could flood you with proposals. Each tier has a share cap, proposals are grouped per tier in "Needs you", and a tier auto-pauses when unreviewed proposals pass a threshold.
  • R6 Credential exposure. Pollers run on the runner host only; the existing redaction tests are extended to the budget block.
